Tunely Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

Audio

Tunely uses your microphone only to detect notes and chords while you use it. All audio is processed on your device in real time. Tunely never records, stores or uploads audio. Audio files you choose in "Song to Chords" are analysed on your device and are not uploaded.

Data we do not collect

Tunely has no accounts. We do not collect your name, email, contacts, location or audio.

Purchases

Subscriptions and purchases are processed by Apple. We do not receive your payment details.

Advertising

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers and usage data to show and measure ads, subject to your choices in the consent form and in Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. You can change ad privacy choices in Settings → Ad privacy choices (where available). Tunely Pro removes ads. See Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

Children

Tunely is not directed to children under 13.
